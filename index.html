– papers

    Papers

    Main-track papers of NeurIPS, ICML and ICLR (machine learning) and CVPR, ICCV and ECCV (computer vision), 2020 to October 2026, with an abstract (–). Workshops, co-located events, Tiny Papers, position papers and competition or demo tracks are left out. The list of papers comes from the dblp XML dump of 2026-10-13, plus ICML 2026 from its PMLR volume (the dump did not have it yet); position papers are left out by their "Position:" title prefix as well. Abstracts come from each conference's own pages wherever possible: PMLR (ICML), the NeurIPS proceedings, the ICLR proceedings (2024–2026) and the ICLR virtual site (2020–2023), CVF open access (CVPR, ICCV) and ECVA (ECCV); OpenAlex by DOI for CVPR and ICCV; when dblp writes a title slightly differently from the conference's own listing (formulas, typos, a method name added), the most similar title of the same venue and year (145 pairs, each read); the arXiv version, only when the title is identical, for what remains. OpenReview's API refuses anonymous requests and was not used. Code and project links are removed from abstracts before matching.

    Topics

    Topics were drawn from the most frequent and the fastest-changing title phrases of the six venues, then written as rules (keywords and phrases). A paper counts for a topic when the rule matches its title or its abstract. Words that are generic inside abstracts (dataset, attention, reasoning, agents, fine-tuning, optimization, distillation, unsupervised, face) have stricter abstract rules. For every topic, random papers matched only through the abstract were read, in 2020–2023 and in 2024–2026, to check that the words keep the topic's sense; rules were corrected until the samples held up. The method is the same as in the robotics study.

    Shares and trends

    Shares are standardised to the venue mix of 2024 and 2025 together, so that both ICCV and ECCV are in it and the years compare. A venue that did not meet in a year (ICCV in even years, ECCV in odd years, NeurIPS in 2026) contributes its share from the model, with a 95% interval. The trend is a logistic regression of topic on venue and year over the whole period; a topic is called rising or declining only when significant after Benjamini–Hochberg correction across all topics. "2024 onwards" repeats the test on 2024–2026 alone. Per venue, the trend is a likelihood-ratio test.

    Limits

    • A share counts papers that name a topic, including in passing; it is not a count of papers whose main contribution is that topic.
    • Keyword rules miss papers that describe a topic without its usual words.
    • ICCV meets in odd years and ECCV in even years, so each has three or four years of data and its single-venue trends rest on few points.
    • Abstracts got slightly longer over the years, so a topic has slightly more chances to be named in later years.
    • Conference years follow submission by six to nine months; arXiv runs ahead of all of this.

    Code and tables

    Scripts, topic rules and result tables are kept with the study. Abstract texts are not redistributed; the scripts fetch them from the original sources.
